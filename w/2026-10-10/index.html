The Week That Was
Week to Saturday 10 October 2026 · 7 stories

Norris's call-in, $139,982 of councillor motions, and a 72-year-old renter in Nowra

Unbudgeted councillor motions, a called-in Nowra DA, social housing and an ageing Shoalhaven, the General Manager's recruitment pack, a councillor podcast and a reader survey.

This week I followed the money and the rules. A register of councillor motions shows how much new spending reaches the chamber without a cost or a funding source. A DA call-in shows how little a councillor has to say to take an application off staff.

The other thread is who carries the risk when Council gets this wrong. Two pieces look at older renters, the 12-bed Safe Shelter in Nowra and the land sales now under way, against what the research and Treasury say is coming.

I also looked at what the General Manager's applicant pack said about knowing the law, talked to Cr Ben Krikstolaitis on the podcast, and asked you what you want from me.

Pick of the week · Friday 9 October
Who is spending money that was never budgeted?

Of 235 councillor-initiated items across 45 meetings, 37 commit Council to spending or forgo revenue, and 24 of those carry no dollar figure. Stated costs total $139,982, including $67,040 a year from three pool-hours reversals. Only two motions name a funding source, and Cr Tribe's move to require one was lost 6-7.

Social housing · Tuesday 6 October

What a Social Home Does, and What Shoalhaven Loses Without One

A 2025 study of 15 people moved from NSW, Queensland and Tasmanian waiting lists found the home came first and health, work and family followed, though the authors say housing alone is not enough. None were from the Shoalhaven. Locally, Safe Shelter's 12 beds sit on land Council voted 7-6 on 28 July to start reclassifying and selling.

Ageing population · Thursday 8 October

She’s 72, She Rents in Nowra, and Treasury Has Just Priced Her Odds

Treasury's 2026 Intergenerational Report finds two in three retirees who rent privately live in poverty. At the 2021 Census Shoalhaven's median age was 48 against 39 for NSW, with 28 per cent aged 65 or over. I argue the 2035 plan names the problems but sets no targets, and I could find no ageing strategy.

Councillor conduct · Friday 9 October

Councillor Norris Called In a DA. His Relative Objected To It.

On 24 September Cr Matthew Norris moved to call in DA2026/1972, a 7-Eleven-anchored centre at East Street, Nowra, citing the public interest; it carried 8-4. The only public submission was an objection from a close relative. No interest was declared on the night; a post-meeting declaration followed my question. Nothing establishes he knew of the objection beforehand.

Governance · Saturday 10 October

Integrity Culture Starts With Knowing the Law: What the Mandarin Says, What ICAC Is Finding, and What Our Council Record Shows

I make no allegation of corrupt conduct. The June 2025 General Manager applicant pack lists local government experience as desirable, not essential, and quotes a version of section 335 of the Local Government Act that does not match the current text, possibly an older version. I draw the inference, not a finding, that knowing the law first-hand is a weak point.
